How to send

From within Pakistan

  • Raast or IBFT from your banking app, using the IBAN above — usually instant.
  • JazzCash or Easypaisa: choose “Send to bank account” and enter the IBAN or account number.

From abroad (remittance)

  • Use a licensed money-transfer service such as Wise, Remitly, Western Union, WorldRemit, Taptap Send or ACE Money Transfer.
  • Choose Bank deposit to Pakistan and enter the account title and IBAN.
  • Set the purpose of payment to Gift.
  • Or send an international (SWIFT) transfer from your own bank using the SWIFT code.

Please use banks or licensed transfer services only, never unofficial channels such as hawala or hundi. Rates and fees vary between services, so it's worth comparing them before you send.
