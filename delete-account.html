Delete your account

You can request deletion of your Pilgrim Companion account and associated personal data at any time. You can do this in the app or contact us if you no longer have access to it.

Delete your account in the app

Open Pilgrim Companion, find the Delete Account option, and follow the confirmation steps. You may need to sign in again to verify that the account belongs to you.

Request deletion without the app

If you have uninstalled the app or cannot access your account, send a deletion request to our support team.
