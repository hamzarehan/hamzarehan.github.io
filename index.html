About Pilgrim Companion

Pilgrim Companion provides step-by-step Umrah and Hajj guidance, duas, sacred-place information, gate navigation and tools that help pilgrims stay connected with their group.

Google and Apple sign-in allow pilgrims to securely access their Pilgrim Companion account across supported devices.
