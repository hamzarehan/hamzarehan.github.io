Principles behind every app

About

Assalamu alaikum. I'm Hamza.

I'm a mobile developer from Pakistan working across iOS, Android and the web. Most of what I build falls into two groups: Islamic apps that make worship and learning easier, and problem-solving tools that fix something I — or people around me — found frustrating.

I design, build and maintain every app here. Feedback goes directly to me, so the person reading your message is also the person working on the next improvement.
