Visual Shield / Privacy policy

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 October 2026

Summary: Visual Shield processes visual content locally on your device. It does not create an account, collect analytics, show advertising, or upload captured image and video pixels to Visual Shield servers.

What Visual Shield processes

Browser extension

The extension analyses frames from supported web videos and images inside your browser to detect the categories you select, such as people and faces. The analysis and concealment process runs locally on your device. Image and video pixels are not uploaded to Visual Shield servers.

For certain cross-origin images that the browser prevents a page canvas from reading, the extension may request the same image URL already displayed by the website so that it can analyse that image locally.

Android application

When you start protection, Android asks for consent to capture the screen. Captured frames are analysed in memory on your device. They are not saved, recorded, uploaded, or shared by Visual Shield. Capture stops when you stop protection.

iOS application

Photos, videos, and camera previews you choose are processed locally. A filtered result is saved or shared only when you request that action.

Information stored on your device

Visual Shield stores settings such as enabled filters, effect, strength, performance mode, presets, and per-site or per-app rules. Browser-extension settings are stored using the browser's local extension storage. Visual Shield does not operate an account or cloud-sync service.

Information we do not collect

  • No account or identity profile
  • No browsing-history database
  • No advertising identifiers
  • No analytics or behavioural tracking
  • No sale or sharing of personal information
  • No face recognition or identity matching; faces are detected, not identified

Third-party components

The browser extension includes local machine-learning models and MediaPipe runtime files packaged with the extension. On Android, supported detection features may use Google ML Kit through Google Play services; Google Play services may download model components. Visual Shield does not send captured screen content to Google or other parties.

Permissions

PermissionPurpose
Website access (browser)Find supported videos and images and draw the selected concealment effect.
Local storage (browser)Save extension settings and per-site rules on the device.
Screen capture (Android)Analyse visible content after the user explicitly starts protection.
Display over other apps (Android)Draw concealment above the selected content.
Notifications (Android)Show that protection is active and provide a Stop action.
Usage access (Android, optional)Apply per-app rules.

Data retention and deletion

Visual content analysed in memory is discarded during normal operation and is not retained by Visual Shield. You can remove locally stored settings by resetting the application or extension, clearing its data, or uninstalling it.

Children's privacy

Visual Shield is a general-purpose privacy tool and does not knowingly collect personal information from children or adults.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version and revision date will be published on this page.
