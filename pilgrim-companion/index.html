Pilgrim Companion

A trusted companion for Hajj and Umrah. Follow each ritual in order, read important duas, explore sacred places and stay connected with your group — with clear guidance whenever you need it.

Features

Everything for the journey, in one app

Step-by-step guides

Clear instructions for every stage of Hajj and Umrah — Ihram, Tawaf, Sa'i and more — in the right order.

Pilgrim AI

Ask about rituals, sacred places, Haram gates and app features and get clear, source-aware answers.

Ziyarat & historic places

Discover meaningful places across Makkah, Madinah and Taif, with their history and significance.

Authentic duas

Essential supplications in Arabic with translations in your language, for every key moment.

Madinah & Haram guidance

Prepare for Masjid an-Nabawi and the Rawdah, visit etiquette and help navigating Haram gates.

Group Locator

Create or join a group and see your family or companions on a live map during busy moments.

Ask a Scholar

Send a religious question through the app when you need guidance from a qualified person.

Sync across devices

Sign in with Apple or Google to keep bookmarks and progress — or continue as a guest.
